These pages are part of an effort to create, after consuming for so long. Pieces are about my interests and others people's - do expect guest papers. Needless to say, no LLMs were used in the composition of the articles; not even to correct them. Thus, typos and poor syntax are also to be expected.

Consider everything on this site to be and remain in the public domain. These pages are sadly not yet served by a self-hosted webserver but by a Github and Vercel CI/CD pipeline. To generate them, I used agentic coding to craft a barebones static site generator because the datedness and bloat of tools such as Astro or Jekyll meant that I had no control nor understanding of their inner workings.
